Meet Solana

Born in the Summer of Love. Still carrying that same magic today.

Born in 1969

More Than Just a Vintage Kombi

Solana is more than just a vintage Kombi - she's a time machine. With her iconic 1969 curves and a heart that hums, she's the ultimate blend of retro charm and big-day elegance.

Dressed in burnt copper orange with a crisp white roof and retro roof rack (perfect for floral arrangements), she's lovingly restored to keep her authentic charm intact.

The copper tones pop beautifully against the blue of the Pacific and the green of the vineyards - making her the most photogenic guest at your wedding.

Classic, cool and unforgettable. And there's something about that burnt copper glow against a white dress that just works.
