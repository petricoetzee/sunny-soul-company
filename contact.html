Common Questions

Things People Often Ask

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as you know your date! Waiheke Island is popular and Linda's calendar fills up quickly — especially for summer weddings (November–March). The sooner you reach out, the better.

Do you travel to other locations?

Linda is based on Waiheke Island and primarily celebrates weddings here. Reach out to discuss your specific location — she loves a creative conversation!

Can I hire the Kombi without a celebrant?

Absolutely. Solana is available to hire independently for wedding transport, photo shoots, or any special occasion. Kombi hire is $280/hour with a minimum 3-hour booking.

What's your celebrant style?

Relaxed, natural, and deeply personal. Linda doesn't do scripts or clichés — she crafts ceremonies that sound like you, full of your stories and what actually matters. No two ceremonies are the same.

How do I secure my date?

For Kombi hire, a 30% deposit secures your date. For celebrant services, get in touch to discuss — Linda will guide you through the process from first chat to signing day.
