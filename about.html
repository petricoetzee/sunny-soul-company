My Story

Celebrant, Kombi Owner & Waiheke Soul

Hi — I'm Linda Deverell, your Marriage & Civil Union Celebrant and the proud owner of Solana, a 1969 classic Kombi van, based right here on beautiful Waiheke Island.

As a celebrant, I help easy-going couples create days that are big on meaning and low on stress — weaving your stories and rituals into a ceremony that feels authentic and true.

My vibe is relaxed and natural meets professional pro — and my approach is simple: slow down, breathe, and honour what matters.

I'm blessed to call this beautiful island of Waiheke my home — and enjoy nothing more than sharing its secret corners with people in love.

And Then There's Solana

A 1969 Kombi Named After the Sun

Solana means "sunshine" or "sunny place" — and she embodies everything The Sunny Soul Company stands for. Born in the Summer of Love and still carrying that same magic, she found her home on Waiheke Island.

She's been lovingly restored to keep her authentic charm — and she brings something truly special to every wedding she attends. That burnt copper glow against a white dress, the harbour blue, the vineyard green — she was made for this island.

She's not just a ride — she's the ultimate backdrop for your first photos as a married couple.

Island Life

Why Waiheke?

Waiheke Island is one of the most magical places in New Zealand — 35 minutes from the Auckland CBD yet worlds away. Rolling vineyards, hidden beaches, world-class restaurants, and a community of people who chose beauty over the rush.

For couples planning a Waiheke Island wedding — from Cable Bay to Mudbrick, from Oneroa to the secret coves only locals know — I'm the guide who can help you find your perfect spot and make your ceremony feel like the island itself is celebrating with you.
