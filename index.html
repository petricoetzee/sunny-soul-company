Meet Linda

Relaxed & Natural Meets Professional Pro

Hi — I'm Linda Deverell, your Marriage & Civil Union Celebrant and the proud owner of Solana, a 1969 classic Kombi van, based right here on beautiful Waiheke Island.

I help easy-going couples create days that are big on meaning and low on stress — weaving your stories and rituals into a ceremony that feels authentic and true.

I don't just show up to read the lines. I'm there to help you breathe through the nerves and soak in the magic of the day.

I'm blessed to call this beautiful island home — and I enjoy nothing more than sharing its secret corners with people in love.
